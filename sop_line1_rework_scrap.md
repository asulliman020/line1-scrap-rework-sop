## Purpose
Keep fixable rework separate from permanent scrap on Line 1 so daily scrap reports show real material waste.
### Steps for Line 1 QA Technicians
1.	Check each part coming off Line 1 using the standard gauge checklist (WI-QA-102).
2.	Place any failed part into the red bin at the QA bench so it does not get packed.
3.	Decide if the part can be fixed by re-forming or deburring:\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a. Fixable: Put a Yellow Tag on it and move it to the Rework Rack.\
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;b.	Not fixable: Put a Red Tag on it and drop it in the locked Scrap Bin.
5.	Open the QMS screen on the floor terminal (QMS Portal > Line 1 > Shift Log) and pick your shift and part number.
6.	Enter fixable parts under code REWORK-REC (type the count and defect reason). Never add rework to the scrap box.
7.	Enter permanent scrap parts under code SCRAP-PERM (type the count and defect reason).
8.	Count both bins 15 minutes before shift end, confirm the numbers match the screen, and click Submit for Shift Lead sign-off.
